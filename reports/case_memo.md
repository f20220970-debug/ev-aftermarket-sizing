# Case memo: India's electric two-wheeler aftermarket

**Question.** How large is the servicing and battery-replacement market for India's electric two-wheelers (E2Ws), how fast will it grow, and where could an independent service business play?

**Answer.** About **₹870 Cr in FY26**, rising to about **₹5,600 Cr by FY31** in the base case (45% a year); **₹3,600–8,200 Cr** across bear and bull scenarios.

**How it was sized.** Bottom-up from vehicles sold each year (Vahan-based sources: 2.5 lakh in FY22 to 11.5 lakh in FY25, about 14.8 lakh estimated for FY26), turned into a fleet in use after 3% yearly scrappage. Vehicles past their free first year pay for service and wear parts (₹2,000/3,000/4,000 a year in bear/base/bull, within the ₹2,000–5,000 reported for e-scooters). Battery replacements are added as each year's sales reach age six (40/50/60% replace, ₹55,000 pack falling 7% a year).

**What drives it.**
- The fleet: about 16 million E2Ws in use by FY31 (base), up from 4.4 million in FY26.
- A battery wave from FY28, when FY22's scooters reach age six; batteries are a third of the FY31 base market.
- Spend per vehicle matters more than future sales growth: moving spend from ₹2,000 to ₹4,000 changes FY31 by ₹2,200–3,000 Cr; moving growth from 10% to 30% changes it by ₹800–1,600 Cr, because most of FY31's fleet has already been sold.

**Where to play.** Independent garages could address about ₹1,400 Cr by FY31 (assuming a 25% share). The most attractive entry points are battery diagnostics and refurbishment, fleet contracts with delivery and rental operators, and multi-brand doorstep service where OEM networks are thin (to verify).

**Risks.** Longer-lasting batteries, OEM lock-in through software and parts, slower sales growth (11% in CY2025), and data gaps (FY26 sales estimated; no published average spend per EV).

**Next steps.** Survey ~100 owners on annual spend, interview ~10 garages and OEM service centres, obtain battery degradation data from an OEM or fleet operator, then replace the assumed inputs and re-run the sensitivity grid.
